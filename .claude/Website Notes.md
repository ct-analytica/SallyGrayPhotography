## Old About Me Section
### Hi There!
I’m Sally Gray, and I am obsessed with finding and capturing exceptional photos. With almost 10 years of industry experience, I bring an arsenal of posing guidance, technical skill, and unshakable scrappiness you want in the person tasked with capturing dynamic, fast moving days.

My style blends digital and film images seamlessly, with rich color, true to life skin tones, and expert use of natural light.

Keep scrolling to learn more!

### I Got It From My Mama
I got a lot from my mother, actually. My respect for nature, my love for stray animals, and my passion for honest photography. When I first picked up a camera, I looked to old family photos for inspiration and guidance. What I found astonished me.

While my mother was never a professional, she has always been able to capture special images. She knew the intrinsic value of a photograph, and had the unobtrusive eye to capture beautiful moments with grace.

I’ve spent my career developing an eye for imagery that evokes family photo nostalgia in a refined and elevated experience. All are welcome here.

### My Ethos
#### The three values that govern my work
One: Capture exquisite images that showcase poised beauty and raw humanity in equal parts.

Two: Integrate seamlessly into the people I am serving. Like a really fun, professional but spunky fly on the wall. Be safe, be true, have fun.

Three: Observe, observe, observe. The best moments often slip by in the quiet space between poses and timeline benchmarks. Observe, compose, and capture what is often overlooked, yet always cherished.
