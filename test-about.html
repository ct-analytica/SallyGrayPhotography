About Sally

Hi There, I'm Sally Gray

I'm obsessed with finding and capturing exceptional photos — and I'd love to tell you why.

I'm Sally Gray, and I am obsessed with finding and capturing exceptional photos. Based in Brevard, NC, I bring almost 10 years of industry experience — an arsenal of posing guidance, technical skill, and unshakable scrappiness you want in the person tasked with capturing dynamic, fast-moving days.

Where It Started

I Got It From My Mama

I got a lot from my mother, actually. My respect for nature, my love for stray animals, and my passion for honest photography. When I first picked up a camera, I looked to old family photos for inspiration and guidance. What I found astonished me.

While my mother was never a professional, she has always been able to capture special images. She knew the intrinsic value of a photograph, and had the unobtrusive eye to capture beautiful moments with grace.

I've spent my career developing an eye for imagery that evokes family photo nostalgia in a refined and elevated experience. All are welcome here.

My Ethos

The Three Values That Govern My Work

One

Capture

Capture exquisite images that showcase poised beauty and raw humanity in equal parts.

Two

Integrate

Integrate seamlessly into the people I am serving. Like a really fun, professional but spunky fly on the wall. Be safe, be true, have fun.

Three

Observe

Observe, observe, observe. The best moments often slip by in the quiet space between poses and timeline benchmarks. Observe, compose, and capture what is often overlooked, yet always cherished.
