About Sally

Storyteller.
Observer. Human.

"Wildly disinterested in content creation. Entirely obsessed with the human experience."

I'm Sally Gray — a Western North Carolina wedding and lifestyle photographer based in Brevard, NC, with almost 10 years behind the camera. I bring an arsenal of posing guidance, technical skill, and the unshakable scrappiness you want in the person tasked with capturing your dynamic, fast-moving day.

My style blends digital and film seamlessly — rich color, true-to-life skin tones, and an eye trained on natural light. Whether it's your wedding day, an afternoon with your family, or a quiet portrait session, I want you to forget I'm there, and feel everything when you look back.
